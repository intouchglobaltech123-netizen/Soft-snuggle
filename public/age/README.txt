A photo here replaces the drawn baby on that Shop by age card.

Name it after the age group and the card picks it up on the next page load:
  newborn.webp      0-3 months
  little-one.webp   3-6 months
  sitting-up.webp   6-9 months
  on-the-move.webp  9-12 months

Cut the background out and save as PNG or WebP with transparency, around
1000px tall. A card with no photo keeps its illustration, and only the drawn
ones offer the colour swatches: a photograph of a real garment should not be
recoloured into a shade you do not sell.
